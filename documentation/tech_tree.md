# R&D Intelligence: The Agency Tech Tree

The CTO manages the Agency's long-term capabilities through the Tech Tree. Research is funded by "Research Points" (RP) earned through successful missions and time-based generation.

## 🛠️ Research Mechanics
- **Earning RP:** Points are granted upon mission completion based on difficulty and Technical skill of assigned assets.
- **Node Cap:** If 50 nodes exist and are all researched, RP accumulation pauses until new directives are issued.
- **Unlock Requirements:** Many nodes require specific Agency Levels or prerequisite research.

## 🌿 Core Research Branches

### 1. Ordnance & Equipment (Weapons)
- **Small Arms Tuning:** +5% Combat for Pistols.
- **Suppressor R&D:** Reduces Heat generated by Wetwork missions.
- **Custom Loadouts:** Unlocks the ability to equip assets with specific gear.

### 2. Infrastructure & Operations (Buildings)
- **Advanced Blueprinting:** Unlocks larger building tiers (Warehouses, Skyscrapers).
- **Clean Rooms:** Unlocks Cyber-Heist missions with higher difficulty.
- **Front Business Optimization:** Reduces the "Visibility" of your physical HQ.

### 3. Personnel Techniques (Recruits)
- **The Method:** Unlocks "Senior" training modules.
- **Deep Cover Training:** +10% Subterfuge on Social Infiltration.
- **Psychological Conditioning:** Reduces Morale decay during long missions.

### 4. Corporate Expansion (Departments)
- **CLO Integration:** Unlocks the Legal & Heat management hub.
- **CMO Synergy:** Increases the quality of recruits in the daily pool.
- **CISO Protocols:** Protects Agency Capital from rival player raids (PvP).
