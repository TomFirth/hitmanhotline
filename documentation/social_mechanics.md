# Social Virality & Agency Expansion Program

To accelerate growth and reward word-of-mouth marketing, the Agency OS includes a robust recruitment referral system.

## 🤝 Agency Referral Program

Every CEO is issued a unique **Expansion Uplink** (Referral Code). Inviting new directors to the shadow network provides mutual benefits.

### 🎁 Tiered Incentives
| Milestone | Reward for Referrer | Reward for Recruit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Successful Link** | $1,000 Signing Bonus | $500 Starter Capital |
| **5 Invites** | "Shadow Recruiter" Badge | - |
| **10 Invites** | **Exclusive Asset:** "The Headhunter" (Unique Staff Type) | - |
| **25 Invites** | Private Lair Decor: "Gold-Plated Red Phone" | - |

> [!NOTE]
> **"The Headhunter"** is an exclusive support staff member that increases the base stats of all future market recruits by 10%. This asset cannot be hired via the market.

### 🛡️ Anti-Fraud Protocols
To prevent "Ghost Directorships" (fake accounts) and spamming, the following checks are enforced:
1. **Email Hash Database**: Every invited email is hashed and stored. If an invite is sent to an email already in the system, it does not count as a new recruitment task.
2. **Account Maturation**: Referrer rewards are only disbursed once the recruit completes their first "Wetwork" mission.
3. **Device Fingerprinting**: Prevents multiple accounts from being created on the same local machine to farm bonuses.

## 🔗 Viral Loop & UX Systems

Beyond direct emails, the OS uses modern social triggers to encourage organic growth.

### 1. The "Streamer Uplink" (Public Invite Links)
Direct links (e.g., `hitmanhotline.com/join/DIRECTOR_TOM`) allow for high-volume recruitment.
- **Conversion Tracking**: The dashboard tracks "Uplink Clicks" vs. "Successful Filings."
- **Influencer Caps**: To prevent streamers from breaking the game's economy, pure financial rewards are **capped at 50 recruits**.
- **Prestige Conversion**: Beyond 50 recruits, bonuses are converted into **Prestige** and **Cosmetic Tokens** (e.g., unique UI themes or avatar skins).

### 2. "Classified" Bragging Rights
- **Mission Debriefs**: Upon a "Critical Success," CEOs get a "Generate Secure Dossier" button. This creates a stylised image of the mission result for sharing on Discord or Twitter.
- **Agency Leaderboards**: Public URLs for your "Agency Filing" allows others to view your roster, wealth, and "Wanted Star Rating" from the outside.

### 3. Cooperative Contracts
- **Joint Operations**: Referring a friend unlocks "Duo Contracts"—special missions where both agencies must assign 1 asset. Payouts are 2x higher than standard contracts.
- **Friend Awareness**: The "Internal News Feed" occasionally mentions friend activities: *"Director [FriendName] has just expanded to the European Sector."*

## 📈 Improving Word-of-Mouth UX

- **One-Click Invites**: Integration with standard sharing APIs for mobile users.
- **Red Phone Alerts**: "A new potential director has been detected in your sector. Send an invitation uplink?"
- **The "Welcome Wagon"**: If a user joins via a referral, they appear in the Referrer's **"Human Capital" (HR)** as a "Protected Affiliate," allowing for minor resource trading (Intel/Gear).
