# Hitman Hotline - General Game Concept

## Overview
Hitman Hotline is a business management simulation game where you play as the CEO of a "Hitman Startup." Inspired by classic web games like Planetarion and Counter-Strike Manager, combined with the base-building and management aspects of Evil Genius and Football Manager.

## Core Loop
1. **Recruit:** Hire hitmen and support staff from the market or via scouts.
2. **Train:** Improve your staff's skills to increase mission success rates.
3. **Assign:** Send hitmen on missions (Wetwork, Intel, etc.).
4. **Manage:** Handle admin tasks, sponsors, and office upgrades while missions are in progress.
5. **Scale:** Grow from a one-room operation to a global shadow agency.

## Humour and Tone
The game maintains a "subtle humour" tone—darkly comedic, corporate absurdity meeting the world of professional assassination. Think corporate KPIs for "body disposal efficiency."

## Staff Types
### Hitmen (The Assets)
- Primary revenue generators.
- Can specialise as killers, hackers, social engineers, etc.
- Risk: Captured (not killed), defecting, or being traded.

### Support Staff
- **HR:** Manages recruitment and staff satisfaction.
- **Secretary:** Handles admin tasks and mission briefings.
- **Trainers:** Improve Hitman skills.
- **Marketing:** Finds better sponsors and financial support.
- **Handlers:** Improve mission coordination and success chances.
- **PR:** Manages public heat and agency reputation.
- **Therapists:** Keep hitmen sane and prevent defection.
- **Scouts:** Find better/younger recruits in the market.

## Key Features
- **Real-time Missions:** Missions take real time (minutes to days).
- **Sponsorships:** Daily/weekly contracts providing gear (knives to rifles) and cash.
- **The Market:** An auction/trading house for buying/selling staff with other players.
- **PvP:** Clash of Clans style intel raids or resource theft between agencies.
- **Regional Missions:** Pay for flights (Economy vs First Class impacts hitman morale/readiness).
