# Agency Onboarding & Starter Tasks

New CEOs begin their journey with a lean operation and the opportunity to double their initial capital through a series of "First Week" objectives.

## 🏁 Initial Setup
- **Starter Capital**: $5,000.
- **Roster**: 1 Elite Asset (Agent 47). No support staff.
- **Goal**: Establish agency identity and operational baseline.

## 📋 Starter Tasks (Bonus Capital)
CEOs can earn an additional **$5,000** by completing these foundational tasks. Each task awards **$1,000** instantly upon completion.

| Task | Objective | Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Corporate Identity** | Set your Agency Name in the Account Hub. | $1,000 |
| **First Strike** | Authorise and start your first mission. | $1,000 |
| **Executive Oversight** | Complete 1 CEO Admin Task (Red Phone). | $1,000 |
| **Shadow Networking** | Invite a friend to join the network. | $1,000 |
| **Market Awareness** | Scout the recruitment pool and hire your first Support Staff. | $1,000 |

## 🚀 Progression Tips
1. **Don't spend it all at once**: Keep enough capital to cover your first "Monday Settlement" (Salaries).
2. **Prioritise Support**: Use your bonus capital to hire a **Trainer** or **HR** specialist early.
3. **Intel is King**: Early missions should focus on Intelligence to unlock better sponsorships.
