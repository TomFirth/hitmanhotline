# Multiplayer & PvP: Corporate Espionage

The "Multiplayer" component of Hitman Hotline is built on the philosophy of **Shadow Rivalries**. Agencies do not engage in direct combat; instead, they sabotage, infiltrate, and loot one another through the "Corporate Espionage" hub.

---

## 📊 Global Leaderboards
A public "Transparency Report" for all registered agencies.

- **Metric**: Total Agency Wealth (Liquid Balance + Asset Value).
- **Format**: Paginated table (50 per page).
- **Fields**: Agency Name, Entity Type, Registration Number, Total Capital, Prestige Level.
- **Privacy**: Agencies can pay a "Dark Web Proxy" fee to obfuscate their exact wealth for 24 hours.

---

## 🕵️ PvP: Corporate Espionage
Players can authorise "Raid Contracts" against other agencies appearing on the leaderboard.

### 1. Infiltration Types
- **Intel Raid**: Duplicate a random Tech Tree node from the victim.
    - *Outcome*: Thief gains the tech; victim keeps it (it is essentially "Reverse-Engineered").
- **Asset Headhunt**: A high-risk operation to poach a rival operative.
    - *Risk*: Extremely low success chance (1-3%).
    - *Failure*: If the headhunt fails, the agent may be captured by the victim agency and held for ransom.

### 2. Defences (The CISO Loop)
- Agencies can purchase "Security Infrastructure" (Firewalls, Counter-Intelligence) to reduce the success rate of incoming raids.
- Defensive successes generate "Intel Fragments" that can be used to track the identity of the attacker.

---

## 🏆 The "Grand Trophy" Mechanic (Reverse Old Maid)
Unique multiplayer items that provide significant global buffs.

- **The Prototype**: A unique, high-value asset/item that grants `+20% Mission Success Rate` or `Zero-Risk Missions` while held.
- **Generation**: Players can "Initialise Prototype" via the Tech Tree at a massive cost.
- **The Loop**: Unlike "Old Maid," you **want** to keep the Prototype. However, its presence makes your agency "Visible" on the Global Intel Map, making you a prime target for theft.
- **Theft**: If an agency successfully raids you while you hold a Prototype, they take it.

---

## 🎖️ Trophy Cabinet & World Wonders
Players can create or steal unique "C-Suite Trophies" that provide passive boosts.

- **The Golden Ledger**: `+10% Cash from all Missions`.
- **The Silent Server**: `-50% visibility to rival scans`.
- **The HR Peace Treaty**: `Permanent +5 Morale`.
- **Tracking**: A "Global Trophy Tracker" shows where every unique item is currently held, encouraging a constant cycle of theft and retaliation.
