# CEO Admin Tasks (The "Busy Work" Loop)

To keep the user engaged during multi-day missions, the Agency OS provides small, humorous "CEO Admin Tasks." These are low-stakes decisions that reinforce the corporate-absurdity tone.

## Task Categories

### ⚖️ HR & Personnel Disputes
Small inter-office dramas that require the CEO's "wisdom."
- **"The Missing Stapler":** An operative claims a junior asset stole their favorite 'silenced' stapler.
    - *Option A:* Buy a new one ($50).
    - *Option B:* Mandatory office-wide frisking (+1 Heat).
    - *Option C:* Tell them to "handle it" (Morale -5%).
- **"Dress Code Violation":** Agent 47 keeps wearing the same suit every day.
    - *Option A:* Enforce "Casual Friday" (Morale +5%, Tech -2%).
    - *Option B:* Sponsor a new wardrobe ($500, Prestige +2).

### 🧾 Financial & Expense Reports
Approving or denying ridiculous business expenses.
- **"Weaponised Umbrella":** A researcher wants to expense a 'self-deploying tactical umbrella.'
    - *Option A:* Approve ($200, Tech +5).
    - *Option B:* Deny (Save cash).
- **"Extravagant Lunch":** A handler took a target to a 5-star dinner "for research."
    - *Option A:* Sign off ($1000, Intel +10).
    - *Option B:* Deny (Morale -10%).

### 🛡️ Legal & Compliance (CLO Office)
Dealing with the "paperwork" of being a shadow agency.
- **"Noise Complaint":** Neighbors heard "suppressed pops" at 2 AM.
    - *Option A:* Send a gift basket ($100, Heat -5).
    - *Option B:* Threaten them (+10 Heat, but free).

### 📣 Marketing & Brand (CMO Office)
- **"Agency Motto":** Marketing wants to change the slogan.
    - *Option A:* "We Hit Different" (Prestige +5).
    - *Option B:* "Customer Satisfaction Guaranteed... or Else" (Heat +2).

### 👨‍💼 HR & Personal Life
Handling the "human" in Human Resources.
- **Life Events:** Agents may want raises, or require leave for marriages, honeymoons, or family crises.
- **HR Inbox:** Permits or denies holiday requests. Decisions appear as News/Email alerts.
- **Logistics Costs:** Training is not just time; it includes resource costs (e.g., ammunition rounds per week).

## Mechanics
- **Frequency:** A new task appears every **12–24 hours**. This encourages a once-a-day "Daily Briefing" ritual.
- **Active Monitoring:** Urgent "Red Phone" tasks are only generated while the CEO is actively logged into the Agency OS.
- **Notification:** A "Red Phone" or "Inbox" notification in the CEO Hub.
- **Rewards:** Small gains in **Prestige**, **Intel**, **Morale**, or reductions in **Heat**.
- **Beneficial Events:** Tasks are weighted toward beneficial outcomes (e.g., "Found a loose briefcase of cash") to reward the daily login.
- **Persistence:** Tasks expire if not handled within 48 hours, reflecting "missed opportunities."
