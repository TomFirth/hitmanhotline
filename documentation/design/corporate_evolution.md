# Corporate Evolution & Tax Efficiency

As your agency scales from a "backroom startup" to a global shadow conglomerate, you must navigate the complexities of international tax law and corporate filing to maintain maximum liquidity.

## 🏢 Legal Structures & Milestones

Your choice of legal entity dictates your tax rate, administrative overhead, and access to certain funding events.

| Entity Type | Milestones (Limit) | Tax Rate | Admin Overhead | Features |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sole Trader** | < $50k Revenue / < 5 Staff | 20% | Low | Fast setup, high personal liability. |
| **Private Ltd (UK)** | < $500k Revenue / < 50 Staff | 15% | Medium | Protected assets, requires "Auditor" staff. |
| **PLC (Public)** | $500k+ Revenue / 50+ Staff | 10% | High | Access to G20 funding, requires Board of Directors. |
| **Non-Profit (Front)** | Special Research Goal | 0% | Extreme | High heat, used for rapid continental discovery. |

## 🧺 Money Laundering & Front Businesses

Agency revenue from missions is considered "Dirty Cash." To use this cash for official expenses (Legal restructuring, global expansion, PLC dividends) without triggering a **Regulatory Audit**, it must be laundered through Front Businesses.

### 🏪 Front Business Types
CEOs can purchase various legitimate businesses to act as fronts:
- **Dry Cleaners:** Low capacity, low risk.
- **Cat Cafe:** High morale bonus for staff, medium capacity.
- **Tech Consultancy:** High capacity, unlocks passive Research Points (RP), high audit risk.
- **Nightclub:** Extreme capacity, generates high Heat, requires constant security staff.

### 🧩 Laundering Mechanics
- **Dirty to Clean:** Front businesses automatically convert a set amount of "Dirty Cash" into "Clean Official Revenue" every real-time hour.
- **Audit Risk:** Having a high ratio of Dirty Cash to Clean Revenue increases the probability of a "Tax Investigation" news event.
- **Entity Scaling:** A "Sole Trader" can only manage small-scale laundering. Moving to **Ltd** or **PLC** is required to scale your laundry operations.

## 📉 Tax Bracket Efficiency

The Finance Office tracks your current "Tax Exposure." As you approach the upper limit of a bracket (e.g., reaching 49 staff members while being an Ltd), your effective tax rate will spike unless you file for a corporate restructuring.

### 🧩 Efficiency Factors
- **Income Brackets**: Crossing revenue thresholds (e.g., $100k, $1M) triggers a "Regulatory Audit" news event.
- **Staff Count**: Larger teams require more complex payroll and insurance filings.
- **Regional Sectors**: Operating in multiple continents increases "Global Compliance" costs.

## 📊 Financial Visualization
The **Office of the CFO** should display a **Tax Exposure Graph**:
- **Zone Green**: Operating at peak efficiency for your current entity.
- **Zone Amber**: Close to a threshold; restructuring recommended.
- **Zone Red**: Over-threshold; paying maximum penalty tax.

## ⚖️ Strategic Restructuring
Restructuring takes real-time (e.g., 2 days) and involves:
1. **Administrative Fee**: A one-time prestige and cash cost.
2. **Regulatory Lockdown**: Reduced mission frequency during the transition.
3. **Personnel Requirements**: Some tiers require specific staff types (e.g., a Chief Legal Officer for PLC status).
