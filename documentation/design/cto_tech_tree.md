# R&D Intelligence: The Agency Tech Tree

The CTO manages the Agency's long-term capabilities through the Tech Tree. Research is funded by **Research Points (RP)** earned through successful missions and time-based generation via Server Farms.

---

## 🛠️ Research Mechanics
- **Earning RP:** Points are granted upon mission completion based on difficulty and Technical skill of assigned assets.
- **Blueprints:** Advanced nodes are locked behind **Blueprints**. These are rare fragments found only as rewards for "Intel" or "Cyber" missions. You must run non-financial missions to unlock top-tier tech.
- **Node Tiers:** Nodes range from Tier 1 (Junior) to Tier 5 (Executive).

---

## ⚔️ Branch 1: Strike Force (Attack)
Focuses on offensive capability, mission success rates, and heavy infrastructure.

### T1: Field Equipment
- **Suppressed Handguns:** Reduces Heat generation from Wetwork missions by 10%.
- **Tactical Body Armour:** Increases agent survival rate in Combat failures by 15%.

### T2: Mobility & Reach
- **Secure Transport Hub:** Unlocks Vehicle slots for agents.
- **Rapid Insertion Unit:** Reduces mission travel time by 15%.

### T3: Heavy Ordnance
- **High-Velocity Rifles:** +15% Combat effectiveness for Snipers.
- **Sabotage Kits:** Unlocks "Explosive" mission types with high cash rewards.

### T4: Aerial Supremacy (Base Upgrades)
- **Helipad Construction:** Grants access to "International" missions with 2x rewards.
- **Private Air Strip:** Unlocks global mission coverage; reduces duration of missions over 2000 miles by 30%.

### T5: Prototype Weaponry
- **The "Untraceable" Round:** Missions have 0% chance of generating Heat upon success.

## 🛡️ Branch 2: Bastion Protocols (Defence)
Focuses on protecting the base from rival agencies (PvP) and managing the Law (Heat).

### T1: Perimeter Security
- **CCTV Grid:** +5% chance to identify rival player "Scans" or "Intel Raids."
- **Encrypted Local Storage:** Increases the cost for rivals to steal your liquid capital.

### T2: Digital Fortification
- **State-Grade Firewalls:** Reduces success rate of rival "Intel Raids" by 20%.
- **Honeypot Servers:** Successful defensive intercepts generate 50 RP.

### T3: Physical Deterrence
- **Armed Guard Detail:** Unlocks "Guard" staff role to protect facilities.
- **Automated Turrets:** Reduces success rate of rival "Asset Headhunts" by 25%.

### T4: CISO Executive Suite
- **Counter-Intelligence Cell:** Unlocks the ability to see which player is currently scanning your agency.
- **The Black Hole:** Completely obfuscates Agency Wealth on the leaderboards for 4 hours daily.

### T5: Orbital Shielding
- **EMP Arrays:** Defeating a raid has a 5% chance to disable the attacker's Tech Tree for 1 hour.
