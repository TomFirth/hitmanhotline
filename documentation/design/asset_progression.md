# Skills, Progression & Training

## 🎯 Expanded Skill System
Every agent and staff member possesses a unique set of skills that define their field effectiveness and corporate utility.

### Core Skills & Tiers
| Skill | Description | Level Tiers (Lowest to Highest) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marksmanship** | Accuracy with firearms and ranged weapons. | Nerf Gun Novice, Pew-Pew Prodigy, Sharpshooter, Deadeye, One-Shot Wonder |
| **Stealth** | Ability to move undetected and evade security. | Elephant in a Tux, Wannabe Ninja, Shadow Walker, Invisible Whisper, Ghost Mode |
| **Intelligence** | Tactical thinking and intel connection. | Lost Tourist, Amateur Detective, Puzzle Professor, Mind Reader, Chess Grandmaster |
| **Charisma** | Ability to charm, manipulate, and negotiate. | Awkward Turtle, Small Talk Specialist, Smooth Talker, Con Artist, Persuasion Wizard |
| **Tech Savvy** | Hacking, surveillance, and equipment use. | Dial-Up Dinosaur, Ctrl+Alt+Competent, Hacker, IT Wizard, Digital Ghost |
| **Physical** | Stamina and strength for physical challenges. | Pillow Fort Lover, Gym Visitor, Weekend Warrior, Iron Man, Marathon Machine |
| **Disguise** | Ability to change appearance and identities. | Dollar Store Wig, Costume Party Veteran, Master of Many Faces, Shape Shifter, Chameleon King |
| **Explosives** | Competency with bombs and demolitions. | Accidental Firestarter, Matchstick Maverick, Demo Technician, Kaboom Expert, Boom Whisperer |
| **Vehicles** | Skill with various vehicles and maneuvers. | Bumper Car Driver, Sunday Driver, Street Racer, Wheel Wizard, Drift King |
| **Bribery** | Negotiating with minimal resources. | Empty Wallet, Smooth Schmoozer, Street Banker, Grease-Master, Deal-Closer Extraordinaire |
| **Melee** | Hand-to-hand combat and close-range weapons. | Slap-Fight Champion, Street Fighter, Bar Brawler, Hand-to-Hand Hero, Black Belt Brawler |

## 📊 Visualising Progression
- **Skill Badges:** Unlock playful icons as agents level up (e.g., a chameleon for top-tier disguise).
- **Animated Skill Bars:** Humorous animations (e.g., Stealth bar shows an icon disappearing; Marksmanship shows a bullseye being hit).
- **Titles & Nicknames:** Comical titles displayed alongside levels (e.g., "Stealth: Invisible Whisper").
- **Progression Quotes:** Funny descriptions for each level (e.g., "Still confuses smoke grenades with real ones" for low-level explosives).

## 🏋️ Training & Development
Improving skills requires investment and time.
- **Training Costs:** Factor in rounds per week per assassin (more for snipers).
- **Specialisations:** Build new rooms to unlock training for specific skills.
- **Mentorship:** High-level agents with 5-star heat can spend time training new recruits instead of going on missions.

## 🎖️ Agent Achievement System
To reach the next seniority level, agents must complete a set of internal milestones. XP is no longer the only factor.

### Promotion Requirements
| Seniority Target | Milestone Requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Junior → Mid** | 5 Confirmed Kills + 2 Cyber Breaches + 3 Days Training |
| **Mid → Senior** | 7 Confirmed Kills + 5 Cross-Continental Missions + 1 Elite Specialisation |
| **Senior → Executive** | 10 Confirmed Kills + 1 successful "PLC Restructuring" + 10 Days Mentoring |

### ☕ 1:21 Meetings (Manual Promotion)
Promotions are not automatic. When an agent meets their milestones:
1. A notification appears in the **CHRO Office**.
2. The CEO/CHRO must schedule a **1:21 Meeting**.
3. During this meeting, you review their achievements and authorize the pay raise/rank increase.
4. **Negotiation:** Agents may demand a higher "Promotion Bonus" if their loyalty is low.

## 🏆 Leaderboards & Achievements
Competitive rankings for agencies and individual assets:
- **Agency Rankings:** Overall wealth, total skill level, and contracts completed.
- **Asset Rankings:** Individual agent skill levels and specific achievements.
