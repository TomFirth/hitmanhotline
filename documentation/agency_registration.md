# Agency Registration & Official Filings

To deepen the "Corporate Absurdity" of Hitman Hotline, every agency is treated as a government-registered entity (e.g., Ltd, Inc, GmbH). The CEO must manage public records and official filings to remain operational.

## 🏛️ Official Corporate Profile
Every user has a public-facing "Company House" style filing that includes:
- **Registration Number:** A unique, randomised Agency ID (e.g., HH-882-91).
- **Entity Type:** Starts as "Sole Trader," upgrades to "Private Limited Company (Ltd)" at Level 5.
- **Incorporation Date:** The real-world date the agency was first "booted."
- **Registered Address:** A cryptic operational sector (e.g., "Sub-Level 4, Sector 7G").

## 📊 Government Body Data
The agency is monitored by various shadow-government bodies:
- **Tax Brackets:** 
    - *Startup (Under $10k):* 0% Corporation Tax.
    - *Established ($10k - $100k):* 15% Daily Burn for "Regulatory Compliance."
    - *Empire ($100k+):* 25% Burn, but unlocks "Lobbying" to reduce Heat.
- **Staff Audit (0-100 Scale):** An official count of active "Consultants" (Hitmen) vs "Administrative Staff."
- **Public Disclosure:** A summary of "Consultancy Fees" (Mission Rewards) vs "Operational Expenses" (Salaries/Bribes).

## 👤 User Account Management
- **CEO Profile:** View and edit personal Director details (Username, Email, Profile Avatar).
- **Security Clearances:** Multi-factor authentication (flavour-only or real) and session history.
- **Agency Renaming:** Official filing to change the agency name (costs Prestige and Cash).
- **Liquidation:** The option to "Dissolve the Entity" (Reset account).
