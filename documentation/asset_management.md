# Asset & Facility Management

The Agency operates out of physical locations globally. Each building represents a major overhead but provides essential room for growth.

## 🏙️ Building Portfolio
- **HQ Tiers:** From a "Backroom Startup" to a "Global Citadel."
- **International Presence:** Ability to buy buildings in different sectors (London, Tokyo, New York) to unlock regional missions.
- **Room Configuration:** Buildings have a limited number of "Slots" for specialised rooms.

### 🏢 Room Types & Utilities
- **Training Facilities:** Increases XP gain speed for idle staff.
- **Gun Ranges:** Passive Combat skill improvement.
- **Cafeterias/Dorms:** Maintains high Morale for active rosters.
- **Server Hubs:** Generates passive Research Points (RP).
- **Meeting Rooms:** Increases the frequency of beneficial CEO Admin tasks.

## ⚡ Operational Costs
- **Base Rent:** Fixed weekly cost per building.
- **Utility Tax:** Active rooms add to the electricity and security costs.
- **Global Overhead:** International buildings carry higher logistical costs.
