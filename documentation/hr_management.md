# Human Capital & Mental Load Management

Managing an agency is more than just stats; it's about managing the "human" in human resources.

## 🧠 Mental Load & Stress

Field work takes a psychological toll on agents. 

### 📈 Stress Mechanics
- **Mission Burnout:** Every mission increases an agent's **Stress Meter**.
- **Skill Effectiveness:** High stress acts as a negative multiplier. An agent with 80% stress performs at only 20% of their actual skill level.
- **The "Wall":** At 100% stress, an agent refuses to go on missions and has a high risk of immediate defection or "Burnout" news events.

### 🧘 Recovery & Retention
- **Mandatory Corporate Retreats:** The CEO can send agents on vacation. This costs money and time but resets the stress meter.
- **Support Specialists:** Assigning an agent to a **Therapist** (Support Staff) reduces stress passively over time.
- **Exit Interviews:** When an agent defects, the "Exit Interview" report reveals the specific trigger (e.g., "Salary review ignored," "Too many night-shift missions").

## 🤝 Social Dynamics: Synergy & Friction

Agents are social creatures with professional relationships.

### 🧬 Professional Synergy
- **Bonding:** Two agents who complete 5+ missions together gain **Synergy**.
- **Bonus:** +10% Success boost when paired on the same operation.
- **Unique Pairings:** Certain quirks might have hidden synergies (e.g., two "Obsessed with cable management" agents working on a Cyber mission).

### ⚡ Professional Friction
- **Jealousy:** Hiring a high-cost "Elite" 19-year-old may cause a 34-year-old "Veteran" on the same roster to become jealous.
- **Consequences:** The veteran may demand a raise early, increase their stress faster, or have a higher defection risk.

## 📋 The Board of Directors (PLC Tier)

Upon reaching **PLC (Public)** status, the CEO answers to a Board.

### 🎯 Quarterly KPIs
The Board issues set goals every "Financial Quarter" (e.g., 2 real-time weeks):
- **Operational Focus:** "Complete 5 Social missions this quarter."
- **Efficiency Goal:** "Maintain average salaries below $400."
- **Growth Target:** "Increase overall skill points by 20%."

### 📉 Performance Review
- **Success:** Increases **Prestige** and unlocks exclusive funding events.
- **Failure:** Decreases Prestige, making it harder to recruit top-tier talent and potentially triggering a "Leadership Challenge" news event.

## 🎁 Performance Bonuses
The Board of Directors or Parent Companies may issue one-time bonuses for reaching internal KPIs:
- **Financial KPI:** "Hit $1M in laundered revenue." -> Unlocks a $100k "Dividends" payout.
- **Roster KPI:** "Employ 10 agents with 'Invisible Whisper' stealth." -> Unlocks high-tier camouflage gadgets.
